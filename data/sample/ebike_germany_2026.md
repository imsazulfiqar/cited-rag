# Market size
Germany remains Europe's largest e-bike market with an estimated 2.1 million unit sales in 2025, roughly flat on 2024 as the market matures. Trekking and city models make up 46% of units, e-mountain bikes 34%, and cargo bikes 9%.

# Pricing
The average selling price in Germany was €2,950 in 2025, broadly stable year on year. Leasing through employers (Dienstrad-Leasing) is used for an estimated 40% of new e-bike purchases, which supports demand for higher-priced models.

# Consumer barriers
Among German non-owners, upfront cost was the most cited barrier (44%), followed by the belief that a conventional bike is sufficient (31%) and concerns about battery lifetime (24%). Theft was mentioned by 21%.

# Distribution
Specialist bicycle retailers account for around 70% of German e-bike sales by value. Direct-to-consumer online brands grew to an estimated 14% share, mostly in the city and commuter segments.
