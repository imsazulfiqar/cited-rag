# Market size
France recorded an estimated 740,000 e-bike unit sales in 2025, up 4% year on year after a weaker 2024. City and commuter models represent 52% of units, e-mountain bikes 21% and cargo bikes 13%, the highest cargo share of the three markets covered in this series.

# Pricing
The average selling price in France was €2,240 in 2025. National and regional purchase subsidies, which can reach several hundred euros for lower-income households and higher amounts for cargo bikes, reduce the effective price for many buyers.

# Consumer barriers
French non-owners cited upfront cost most often (49%), followed by theft (38%) and safety concerns about cycling in traffic (29%).

# Infrastructure
Investment in protected cycle lanes in Paris, Lyon and Grenoble is the main driver of commuter demand. Cities with continuous protected networks report e-bike commuting shares roughly twice the national average.
