# Market size
The UK e-bike market reached an estimated 410,000 unit sales in 2025, up 9% year on year. Commuter models account for 58% of units, cargo bikes for 11%, and leisure or trekking models for the remainder. Growth is concentrated in London, Manchester and Bristol, where employer cycle-to-work schemes are most widely used.

# Pricing
The average selling price in the UK was £1,850 in 2025, down from £1,990 in 2024 as discounting on entry-level commuter models increased. Premium models above £3,500 held their prices and grew their share of revenue to 22%.

# Consumer barriers
The most cited barrier to purchase among UK non-owners was fear of theft (47%), followed by upfront cost (41%) and lack of secure storage at home (33%). Range anxiety was mentioned by only 12% of respondents.

# Regulation
UK e-bikes are limited to 250W motor power with assistance cutting out at 15.5 mph. A consultation on raising the power limit for cargo bikes was opened in 2025 but no change has been confirmed.
